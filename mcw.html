Stage 1 — Discover the Expressions

Read the festival story and learn six useful expressions. Try to notice what each expression means in context.

Our class is preparing for the school festival. Mina is in charge of the English booth. She asked everyone to take part in a short quiz game. At first, the team could not figure out how to make the activity interesting. They decided to work on a mystery theme. After testing the game, they decided to cut down on long instructions. They hope these changes will make a difference for the students who visit the booth.

1. in charge of

= responsible for something
Mina is in charge of the English booth.

2. take part in

= participate in / join an activity
Everyone can take part in the quiz.

3. figure out

= understand or find the answer to something
We need to figure out the answer.

4. work on

= spend time improving or developing something
We are working on a new game.

5. cut down on

= reduce the amount of something
Let's cut down on the long instructions.

6. make a difference

= have a positive or important effect
Small changes can make a difference.
Tip: Focus on the meaning, not just the Korean translation.
